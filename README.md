# Astroic Adventure

A game made in C++ Raylib. You are a alien which wants to go to his planet and deals with asteroids on his way. If you hit 100 asteroids you win, and if you lose all your lives or get 0 as your score you lose

# How to install (Windows 11/10)

First download the file called Astroic.Adventure.v1.0.Windows.11.x86-64.zip, And right click on the downloaded folder (usually in your Downloads folder) and right click on it. There should be an option called "Extract All" and click that, and extract the zip wherever you want. Find main.exe and double click on it to play the game. NOTE : If you want you can right click on it and click "Show More Options" to create a shortcut and put it in your desktop to play the game by double clicking on the shortcut icon.
